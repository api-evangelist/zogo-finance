When I hear the word “game,” my mind still drifts straight to unlocking Zelda dungeons, tossing bananas on Mario Kart (Toad player for life), and pulling off a perfect trick mode combo on Tony Hawk Pro Skater III. I can still picture the vinyl carpet under my elbows, the N-63 light flickering, and the diplomacy-level negotiations over whose turn it was next. These moments weren’t just about winning - they were about connection.
